every upload is released under the
[Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Public Domain Dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
([Openclipart FAQ](https://openclipart.org/faq)). They are kept here
unchanged; the sample projects scale them and take their outline.
